# P5 Archive Export 1.5.7

Both apps, Mac and menu bar. Universal, signed and notarized.

## New

**Results, Log and Charts are underline tabs.**

The workflow switch at the top (SQL Export, Volume Export, Backup Export) and the
three views inside SQL Export were two identical orange segmented controls, one
above the other, which read as a single row of six. The three views are now
underline tabs over the content they control: the selected one is orange with an
underline, the others grey. The workflow switch is unchanged.

## Fixed

**"Where the volume sits" in the Charts tab is readable again.**

The size-range labels were drawn on top of the bars and of the neighbouring row's
figures. The chart is now drawn as plain rows, the way the HTML dashboard shows
it: the size range, a bar, then the amount and "N jobs · N%". A size range with
no jobs shows an empty bar.
